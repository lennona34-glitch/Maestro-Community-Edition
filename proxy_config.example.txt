# HOW TO GET YOUR NORDVPN SERVICE CREDENTIALS:
# 1. Log in to your Nord Account at: https://my.nordaccount.com/
# 2. On the left sidebar, click "NordVPN" (under Services).
# 3. Scroll down to the "Manual setup" section and click "Set up NordVPN manually".
# 4. Under "Service credentials (manual setup)", you will see:
#       - Username: (a generated string of letters and numbers, NOT your email)
#       - Password: (click Copy to copy it)
# 5. Paste them below and change ENABLE_PROXY=false to ENABLE_PROXY=true.
